# H-Beauty — 48 distinct real makeup photographs
**Project:** same adult model as the supplied H-Beauty reference; Persian RTL luxury salon gallery.  
**State:** SHOOT/ASSET DELIVERY REQUIRED — zero of 48 results have been verified; the present reference portrait is NOT a photograph of each result.

## Non-negotiable photography contract
1. **48 separate makeup applications and 48 separate camera captures of the same model.** Every named look below must genuinely be applied to the model and photographed separately. A duplicated portrait, an AI-generated portrait, changed saturation, renamed image, or makeup swatch does **not** qualify.
2. Identity: preserve natural face, eyes, nose, eyebrows and mouth. No reshaping, liquify, face replacement, beauty filter, heavy pore removal or invented face details.
3. Every photo needs an original camera filename, a **unique individual shot ID**, photographer, capture date, the model's consent for salon publication, and independent human visual approval of identity and actual makeup.
4. Capture in calibrated high-quality light, matching portrait framing. Target 3:4 portrait composition, safe to crop on mobile; full face unobstructed, realistic skin texture, accurate makeup colors. Neutral cream/blush studio background preferred. Consistent lens / camera / white balance across the series.
5. Export each approved photo as visually distinct 3:4 WebP, **minimum 900 × 1200 px**, with careful face-safe cropping and no embedded logo, text, watermark or UI icons. Preserve separate original RAW/JPEG masters; do not commit private originals to the public repository.
6. Treat the makeup execution, model identity and publication rights as separate approvals. Metadata and checksums can catch obvious mistakes, but cannot prove who was photographed or whether makeup was genuinely performed.
7. **No virtual try-on** in this phase. Do not misrepresent AI mockups as real makeup portfolio photography.

## Integration contract
- Strict, unapproved roster: `src/data/makeup-photo-manifest.json`.
- Generated filenames are deterministic: `public/makeup/results/<look-id>.webp`.
- For every completed photo, fill its *actual* shotId, capturedAt (ISO date/time), originalCaptureFile, photographer, and three approval booleans in the manifest. Never prepopulate these fields with fabricated metadata.
- After delivery, run `npm run verify:makeup-photos` to reject missing assets, duplicate filenames / exact duplicate images, tiny exports and unapproved entries. Run identity / near-duplicate visual review separately.
- **Do not replace the current gallery's reference imagery with claimed 48 completed results, change marketing copy to claim real looks, or merge/deploy until all 48 actual images and approvals exist.**
- Review the design at 320, 390 and 768 px, Persian RTL, and dark mode after swapping assets.
